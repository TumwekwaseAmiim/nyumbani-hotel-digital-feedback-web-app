NYUMBANI HOTEL MONTHLY RESET POLICY

Recommended month-end workflow:

1. Manager/System Admin reviews the month's feedback.
2. Print or save:
   - full monthly feedback report
   - category summary
   - complaints and resolutions
3. System Admin opens Settings.
4. Confirm report has been saved.
5. Type RESET MONTH.
6. The app archives an anonymous aggregate summary.
7. Current guest/customer records are deleted locally.
8. Staff accounts remain unless System Admin uses the separate Staff Reset section.

The anonymous monthly archive keeps totals only, not names, phone numbers, emails, room numbers, or guest comments.

For a real online database, do not use browser-only deletion logic. Implement server-authorized retention/deletion policies and backups.
